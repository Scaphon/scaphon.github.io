What this site collects

This site has no forms, no accounts and no advertising or tracking cookies. If we add visitor analytics, we will use a cookieless service that counts page views without identifying you, and we will name it on this page.

When you email us

If you write to staycool@scaphon.com or another Scaphon address, we keep your name, email address and what you send us so we can reply and keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Our email is hosted by a third-party provider, so messages are also stored with that provider under its own terms.

Confidential material

Technical and commercial material we share in a briefing is shared under a non-disclosure agreement, and we treat what you share with us the same way.

Access and correction

Ask us at staycool@scaphon.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, and we will do it.
