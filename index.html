The thesis

AI outgrew the rack.

Why it matters

Every operator is being asked to bet a 30-year building on cooling that lasts one GPU generation.

  • AirOut of headroom.Fans and air handling stopped keeping up with AI racks several GPU generations ago.
  • Cold platesStop at the chip.They take the heat off the processor and its memory. Fans and air still cool the rest of the server.
  • Immersion tanksCool everything.Every repair lifts a dripping server from a bath, a service model hyperscale won't run.
  • The rackBeing redesigned.NVIDIA's Kyber and AMD's Helios both break with the rack layout used since 1965.

Scaphon is built for the hall that has to outlive all of them.

The curve

Chipmakers double rack power every 18 months. Heat, service, space and water have to keep up.

15 kW in 2020. Over 200 kW today. 600 kW, then beyond 1 MW, before 2030. The chart tracks power, the number every chip roadmap leads with. Behind it, every step up the curve concentrates more heat in the same rack, puts more compute behind every repair, needs more floor for power and cooling, and leaves more of that heat to reject outside.

What we're building

A new form factor for liquid-cooled compute, built to replace the rack.

Liquid carries 1,400 to 3,500 times more heat per litre than air, yet every liquid cooling option on the market still leaves the operator a trade: heat left on air, a tank to service, or a facility to rebuild. Scaphon is designed around five commitments instead.

  1. One server out, the rest keep running.A server is replaced without draining a bath or stopping its neighbours.
  2. Hybrid liquid cooling, no air.Every part of the server is liquid-cooled, including the parts cold plates leave to the air.
  3. The cooling changes with the silicon. The building stays.Each GPU generation gets the cooling it needs, and the hall it lands in does not have to be rebuilt.
  4. More megawatts per square metre.Floor density is a primary design target, because power-ready floor is the scarce input.
  5. Dry heat rejection, near-zero water.Return water warm enough for dry coolers with no chillers, and heat in one loop, ready for reuse.
